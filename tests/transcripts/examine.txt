# E: AAAA: XX- then keys (MONITOR_SPEC 6.3). Only one-digit entries here:
# two digits advancing on their own is a tracked ROM bug (TODO.md Review findings).
> F 0100 02FF 00
> F 0201 0201 99

# One digit is a value; CR stores and advances; CR alone advances; '.' exits.
< E 0200\r5\r7\r\r.
E 0200
0200: 00-5
0201: 99-7
0202: 00-
0203: 00-

# Bare E resumes at the address shown when '.' was typed. BS removes a digit;
# BS with no digit does nothing.
< E\r\x081\x082\r.
E
0203: 00-1\x08 \x082
0204: 00-

# Non-hex keys (including '-') are ignored and not echoed; a lowercase digit
# echoes as typed; '.' discards a pending digit.
< E 0205\rzG-\x1Ba\r3.
E 0205
0205: 00-a
0206: 00-3

> D 01FF 01FF
